Safety Regulations

Strictly Prohibited Shipping Items

Under Indian Postal Acts, custom regulations, and international IATA guidelines, the following categories of items cannot be accepted for international shipping transit. Please review carefully before scheduling bookings.

Prepaid Postal Items

Stamped and prepaid envelopes, postcards, or government postal parcels.

Precious Gems & Jewelry

Precious stones, gold, silver ornaments, loose diamonds, or high-value minerals.

Currency & Bearer Cheques

Cash bills, loose coins, uncrossed (bearer) drafts, bank checks, or active securities.

Poisons & Toxins

Hazardous chemical elements, biological toxins, or lethal laboratory agents.

Firearms & Explosives

Weapons, military equipment, live ammunition, firecrackers, or flammable materials.

Radioactive Materials

Hazardous waste, active radioactive elements, or unshielded research isotopes.

Liquor & Raw Food

Alcoholic beverages, open liquor, perishable foods (except sealed snacks under guidance).

IATA Restricted Items

Pornographic materials, items infringing the Indian Postal Act of 1898, or restricted spray aerosols.
