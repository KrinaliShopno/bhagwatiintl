What We Do

Logistics & Courier Services

Air Shipping Services

Our organization specializes in providing excellent Air Shipping Services to corporate and individual clients. These are ideal for transporting cargo, documents, and heavy materials via air network, which is the fastest route for urgent packages. We hold close associations with major airline services to handle bulk goods clearance and deliver on tight deadlines.

Sea Shipping Services

We are actively engaged in providing professional Sea Shipping Services to different ports of the world. Our maritime freight services are availed mainly by companies that require the transit of heavy goods, oversized machinery, or high-volume items where cost efficiency is prioritized over emergency delivery times.

Courier & Document Delivery

Couriers have emerged as one of the most convenient and affordable modes of goods delivery globally. Whether it's a critical document that is a next-day shipping emergency or a less-urgent heavyweight shipment, Bhagwati International provides export coverage, reliability, and real-time visibility. We make the entire export experience simple, assisting you in customs clearance, duties/taxes, and import-export rules.

E-Commerce Supply Chain Solutions

To connect businesses globally, we offer tailor-made E-Commerce Solutions that assist startups and established enterprises in supply chain management, technical support, warehousing, storage, and order fulfillment. We aim to dispatch packages seamlessly using top-tier tracking methods at reasonable operating costs.

Commercial Cargo Services

We are a leading provider of commercial Cargo Services based in India. Our cargo operations are highly appreciated by clients for their strict safety regulations and punctual timelines. We coordinate sea shipping, air freight, and domestic land courier transfers seamlessly under a single invoice.
